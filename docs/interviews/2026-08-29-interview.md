# User Interview Record: Email Phishing Verification

* **Date:** 2026-08-29
* **Duration:** ~10 minutes
* **Participant:** Coworker from internship last summer
* **Context:** Evaluation of everyday decision-making and friction around suspicious email triage.

---

### Current Workaround
When faced with an unclear email, the participant uses extra time to manually re-read the message and attempting to match it against memory from corporate training. If they are still unsure, they default to submitting a report via the built-in Outlook "Report Phish" button or escalating directly to internal IT staff for manual verification.

---

### Interview Questions & Responses

1. **Walk me through the last time you got an email or a text that looked like it might be a scam. What did you do?**
   > *"From what I recalled from the last IT training, I knew to immediately question the email. However, after looking at it for a bit longer, it was harder to tell whether or not it was a phishing email. In the end, we were told to be better safe than sorry, so I reported it for phishing."*

2. **What do you generally do about these today when you aren't sure if a link is safe?**
   > *"Currently, we are told to use the report phish button that is found in Outlook."*

3. **What is the most annoying part of trying to figure out if an email is legitimate?**
   > *"A lot of the emails you can tell are fake. There are a few, however, that look like they are coming from inside the company and I have no clue whether it is real. So, I end up taking extra time or have to bother IT about whether or not it’s real."*

4. **Have you ever paid for an antivirus, installed any app, or used a specific website to help you check these things?**
   > *"I have not. I know our company issued laptops have built in anti-virus, but that’s about it."*

5. **When was the last time this happened? And before that?**
   > *"The last time I remember receiving a phishing email was last week. Then before that, about 1-2 weeks before that. I received a few of them in the last week."*

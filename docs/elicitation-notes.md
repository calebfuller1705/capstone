# Elicitation Interview Notes
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Interviewee:** Corporate Employee

* [F] The user frequently receives emails from external sources.
* [O] "However, after looking at it for a bit longer, it was harder to tell whether or not it was a phishing email."
* [F] "There are a few, however, that look like they are coming from inside the company and I have no clue whether it is real."
* [F] "So, I end up taking extra time or have to bother IT about whether or not it’s real."
